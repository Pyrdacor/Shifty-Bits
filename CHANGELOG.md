# Changelog

## 1.3

- Achievements: 30 goals, listed under AWARDS in the menu and announced in the game when they are reached.
- Non-zero mode: no more blocks with a row or column of four zeros (they ended the game wherever they landed), and
  a few more 1s (55 %). The other modes keep the blocks of their seeds.

## 1.2

- DRIFTWOOD, the fifth song of 1.1, is gone again: back to the four songs.
- Browser on phones and tablets: the first tap starts the sound of the intro (it stayed silent until the second tap).

## 1.1

- New song: DRIFTWOOD, a calm and long piece (about 2:40, six parts) for long games.
- A little secret, for those who know how to type a name on a phone.
- The test mode renders a song once through (`--shot music.wav music5`).

## 1.0

- The first release: Windows, Linux and the browser.
- Five game modes: normal, countdown, score, non-zero and alternate.
- An intro in the style of the Amiga and Atari ST demo scene, with its own chip tune.
- A menu with a mode selector, options that show up where a mode has them, and START, NEW SEED, INTRO and HELP.
- Four songs, sound effects, seeds, screenshots and touch controls in the browser.
